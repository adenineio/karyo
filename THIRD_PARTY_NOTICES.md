## Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1)

Trimmed web fonts the Adenine and Fresh themes draw in, in `src/engine/fonts/` (each folder holds its font's full
licence as `OFL.txt`). Live pages serve them from Karyo itself; standalone explainers carry them inline. They are
subsets (Latin and the symbols Karyo's interface writes) of the upstream variable fonts, limited to the weights the
themes use; as Modified Versions they keep their original names, which none of these fonts reserves.
